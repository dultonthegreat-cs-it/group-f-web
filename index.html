About Hill Academy

Hill Academy is a leading independent school dedicated to fostering academic excellence, creativity, and character. Since our founding, we've served students from kindergarten through 12th grade with a commitment to personalized learning and community engagement.

Our Mission: To inspire students to think critically, act compassionately, and lead with purpose in an ever-changing world.

Why Choose Hill Academy?

  • Small class sizes ensure personalized attention for each student
  • Rigorous curriculum balances academics with arts and athletics
  • Experienced faculty dedicated to student success
  • Strong college placement record with scholarships and advanced placement
  • Safe, inclusive, and supportive community for all learners

Our Programs

Academics

College-prep curriculum with strong STEM, humanities, and arts programs. Small classes ensure personalized feedback and support.

Athletics & Clubs

Varsity sports, intramurals, and 20+ clubs help students discover passions and build leadership skills.

Community Service

Students engage in meaningful service learning and volunteer work to make a positive impact locally and globally.
